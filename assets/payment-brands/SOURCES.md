# Payment provider artwork

Logos identify payment providers; they do not indicate endorsement or guarantee acceptance. Trademarks remain with their respective owners. SVGs/PNGs are bundled locally; no provider logo service receives user account information. Simple Icons artwork is CC0; repository licenses apply to the other source collections and do not transfer trademark rights. Color fills on monochrome Simple Icons marks follow their recognizable provider colors.
